What changed, and why
  1. No card. The inbox fills the content area edge to edge. Panes are split by hairlines only, so there's no double border and no wasted gutter.
  2. Inbox switcher is a dropdown. A full column for a single "Inbox" item left most of the rail empty. The workspace-style switcher at the top lists inboxes and links to settings.
  3. Inbox comes first and is the default. It shows every open conversation, assigned or not. Assigned to me, Unassigned and Breaching soon narrow it down. Counts appear in one place only (they were shown twice). "Breaching soon" turns red when it has items.
  4. Archived / Spam / Trash move to the rail footer. You rarely need them, so they sit out of the way.
  5. Tag creation moves to settings. Tags in the rail are now filters; "Manage" opens Inbox settings.
  6. The CSAT dropdown is gone from the list header. CSAT, status, priority, assignee, tag and address all live in one Filter menu. Active filters show as removable chips.
  7. Rows you can scan. Unread dot, sender, time, subject, preview, then SLA, priority bars, tags and assignee. Hovering shows quick actions (assign, snooze, resolve). The avatar turns into a checkbox for bulk actions.
  8. Thread header has one primary action. Resolve (split button with Close / Spam / Trash) next to Assign and Snooze. The three status/priority/assignee selects become properties in the details panel.
  9. Details panel. Contact, properties, SLA, linked feedback and past conversations, toggled with I. Below 1360px it becomes an overlay.
  10. Addresses filter every view. "All addresses" is the default. Clicking an address narrows the current view (Unassigned, Assigned to me…) to mail sent to that address, and a chip in the list shows it. Helpdesk addresses have a life-buoy icon, Mail addresses an envelope.
  11. Reply box is a single box with Reply / Note tabs. The From picker chooses which address to reply from, defaulting to the one the mail was sent to. A Mail / Support case switch decides what the reply is: switching a plain mail to a support case gives it a ticket number, status, assignee, SLA and a CSAT survey. Cases send with Send & resolve; mail sends with Send.
  12. Empty states do something. With nothing selected you see the keyboard shortcuts. An empty view says "All caught up" and links to the next useful view.
  13. Mobile. Below 1080px the left column becomes a drawer (panel button by the view title). On phones one pane shows at a time: list, then the conversation with a back button, and details as a full-screen sheet. Rows swipe left to resolve and right to snooze. The reply box puts From on its own row, and inputs use 16px text so iOS doesn't zoom.
  14. Keyboard: J/K move, E resolve, A assign to me, R reply, N note, / search, X select, I details.
