1. How a message travels

The domain can be hosted anywhere. The customer adds one MX record, and Frogmail does the rest.

Senderjane@gmail.com → hello@newsite.com
SES inboundMX inbound-smtp.us-east-1; checks spam, virus, SPF, DKIM, DMARC
FrogmailStores the message and matches a route: hello@ → forward(email) + forward(Veerify)
Destinations① Veerify inbox (signed webhook)
② me@frogbyte.io (re-sent “Jane via hello@”)

Spam or a virus means no forward; the message still lands in Veerify under Spam. Replies sent from Veerify go out as hello@newsite.com through the Frogmail SMTP gateway that already handles support replies.
