Brand has the domain, look, people, Marketing, Support, statuses and categories.
Product has its own feedback board and is a filter/tag on Roadmap and Changelog.
With one product, the product layer disappears from the UI, so Veerify looks exactly like today.

Tool Lives on Per product?

Marketing Brand No. One site, one set of mentions and competitors.
