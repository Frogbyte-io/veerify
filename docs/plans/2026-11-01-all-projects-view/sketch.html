Clickable sketch. Open the project switcher (top left) to move between All projects and one project. In All projects, every page shows project chips to narrow down, and every row is tagged with its project.

Decisions

  1. All projects is a place, not a filter. It sits at the top of the switcher (⌘0, projects ⌘1–⌘9). It only appears once you can open 2 or more projects.
  2. Last used is remembered. After login you land in whatever you were in last: All projects or one project.
  3. Project identity everywhere. Each project's colour and initial (from its appearance settings) show as a left edge on inbox rows and roadmap cards, a dot on the timeline and a tag on rows. Tags only appear in All projects.
  4. Chips narrow, they don't switch. They filter within All projects, remembered per page and kept in the URL (?projects=dml,dek).
  5. Only projects you can open. Restricted projects you aren't a member of are left out, so admins and members can see different sets.
  6. The sidebar shows every tool that's on in at least one project. Projects without that tool have greyed-out chips. Marketing and project settings are hidden, with a note saying why.
  7. Full actions in place. Replies go out from the conversation's own project address. Creating something asks for the project, defaulting to the last one used.
  8. The combined inbox is sorted by time, newest first, with no grouping.
  9. The combined roadmap merges columns by status value. A status only one project has gets its own column, marked "Decker only".
  10. Reports will also be combined, with a per-project breakdown, once Reports is built.

The Overview, redesigned

  • Every number has a label, a period and a comparison. No bare "3" or "ideas": "Conversations received · last 7 days · ↑ 14% vs previous 7 days".
  • "Needs you" comes first, and each row is a sentence with one button ("Reply to Marta, the SLA runs out in 12 minutes" → Reply). Most urgent first. When it's empty: "You're all caught up".
  • Colour means good or bad only where there is a right direction. A faster reply time is green. Volume (conversations, posts, votes) is grey, because more isn't better or worse.
  • The project split is a thin bar under each count in the project colours (hover for the numbers), so you see which project drives the number without another table.
  • A projects table with column headers replaces the cards. Problems are red, zeros are grey, and "Inbox off" is said in words. Click a row to switch to that project.
  • Single-project mode uses the same page, just without tags, splits and the projects table.

Still open

  • Is "Needs you" personal or team-wide? The sketch counts everything unanswered. With assignment it could show "assigned to me + unassigned" only.
  • Which numbers matter most to you in "Last 7 days"? Candidates: CSAT, resolved conversations, feedback shipped, roadmap items moved.
  • Muted projects: should muting a project leave it out of the sidebar badge and "Needs you"?
